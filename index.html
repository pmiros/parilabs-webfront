About us

Parilabs is an engineering consultancy founded by engineers who have shipped safety-critical medical devices, industrial control systems, hydrogen energy rigs and connected IoT products.

Our philosophy: build it, measure it, prove it. We prototype early, test rigorously and design with manufacturing and compliance in mind from day one — so the product that works on the bench is the product that passes certification and scales into production.
